你的資料 / Your data

GPS 路線只保存在記錄它的手機上，不會上傳；分享圖片由手機自己合成，預設不含路線、精確時間與錢包位址。
GPS routes stay on the phone that recorded them. Share images are built on the device and by default carry no route, exact start time or wallet address.
