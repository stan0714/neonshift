怎麼玩／How it works

每日任務

8,000 步或 7 小時睡眠，透過 Health Connect 驗證後，一次錢包簽章完成打卡。

Two missions a day, verified through Health Connect, one wallet signature to clock in.

跑步與健走

App 內 GPS 記錄或匯入 Health Connect 的運動，個人最佳可鑄造成成就 NFT。

Record runs and walks with GPS or import from Health Connect; mint personal bests as achievement NFTs.

會進化的跑鞋

Origin → Pulse → Phase → Surge → Zenith，五階免費進化，獎勵倍率隨之提升。

Five free stages of gear; your reward multiplier rises with each one.

週末競技場

質押 tSKR、走完週末，前 30% 平分獎池；結算 hash 上鏈可驗證。

Stake tSKR, walk the weekend, split the pool with the top 30%. Settlement is committed onchain.

藝廊

看看其他玩家的等級、徽章與 PB 收藏；只顯示鏈上公開資料，不含健康數值。

Browse other players' gear and badges — public onchain data only, never health values.

隱私優先

原始健康資料與 GPS 路線只留在手機；伺服器僅保存任務摘要最多 30 天，可隨時刪除。

Raw health records and routes never leave your phone. Summaries are kept for at most 30 days and deletable in-app.

Built for Solana Mobile Seeker (Android 14+). Coming to the Solana dApp Store.
