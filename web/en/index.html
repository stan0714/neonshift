How it works

Daily missions

Hit 8,000 steps, verified through Health Connect, then clock in with one tap.

Runs and walks

Record with in-app GPS or import from Health Connect; pace, splits and personal bests are tracked for you.

Gear that evolves

Origin → Pulse → Phase → Surge → Zenith. Five free stages; your reward multiplier rises with each one.

Weekend Arena

Join the weekend tournament, walk the weekend, and split the prize pool with the top 30%.

Gallery

Browse other players' gear, badges and personal-best collections. Health values are never shown.

Privacy first

Raw health records and GPS routes never leave your phone. Summaries are kept for at most 30 days and can be deleted in the app at any time.
